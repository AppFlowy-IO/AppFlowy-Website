Hey there,

v0.14.3 is out!

Database Forms are here, making it easy to collect customer feedback, employee surveys, requests, RSVPs, and more directly in your AppFlowy databases.

<Video src='https://www.youtube.com/embed/7Qifi-Vf4BY' alt='AppFlowy v0.14.3 Database Form view demo' />

### 🎉 Build forms from your databases

- Build forms from existing database properties.
- Make important questions required.
- Add question descriptions to give respondents more context.
- Preview forms before sharing them.
- Save every submission automatically to the connected database.

### 🌐 Share forms externally

- Publish a form with a public web link and share it with customers, partners, applicants, or anyone outside your workspace.
- Let people respond without creating an AppFlowy account.

### 🔐 Create secure internal forms

- Restrict forms to authenticated workspace members for employee surveys, IT requests, HR workflows, and internal feedback.
- Enable anonymous submissions when you want candid responses without recording identities.

### 📊 Turn responses into insights

Use Chart view to visualize survey results, satisfaction scores, customer feedback, and other form data in the same database. Move from collecting information to organizing responses and analyzing results without transferring data between separate tools.

### 🔥 Upcoming releases

- Database Timeline view.
- Improved Calendar view on desktop.
- Database formulas.
- Database cell undo and redo.
- Database version history.
- Side Peek mode for database records.
